# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility,
and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
sexualized attention, personal or political attacks, trolling, and publishing private information.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
ban participants for behavior they deem inappropriate. Report conduct issues privately through
the repository maintainers. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 guidelines:
correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban according to impact and recurrence.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
